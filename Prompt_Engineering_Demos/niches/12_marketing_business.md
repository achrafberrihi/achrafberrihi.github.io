# Marketing & Business

Prompts for positioning, content and growth.

## 1. Value proposition builder

```text
Product: {product}
Target customer: {customer}
Main alternatives they use today: {alternatives}

Write 3 value propositions, each with: a headline (max 10 words), a one-sentence
explanation, and the proof point that makes it credible. Avoid vague words like
"innovative", "seamless", "cutting-edge".
```

> **Why it works:** comparing to alternatives and banning buzzwords forces specific positioning.

## 2. LinkedIn post writer

```text
Write a LinkedIn post about: {topic}
Author: {role}, audience: {audience}
Goal: {goal} (e.g. attract AI-data clients)

Structure: a hook line that isn't a question, a short story or concrete result,
3 takeaways, and a soft call to action. 150–220 words. No hashtag spam (max 3), no emojis in every line.
```

> **Why it works:** explicit structure and anti-patterns produce posts that read as written by a person.

## 3. Cold email sequence

```text
Write a 3-email cold outreach sequence from {sender} to {persona} at {company_type}.
Offer: {offer}

Email 1: personalized observation + one-line offer + low-friction question (max 90 words).
Email 2 (3 days later): a relevant case result with a number.
Email 3 (7 days later): polite break-up email.
Include subject lines. No false urgency, no fake familiarity.
```

> **Why it works:** short, value-first emails with a clear sequence outperform long pitches.

## 4. Competitor analysis

```text
Compare {my_company} with these competitors: {competitors}.
Use only the information provided below:
{competitor_info}

Build a table: target customer, pricing model, key features, strengths, weaknesses.
Then give 3 positioning opportunities where {my_company} can win, and 1 area to avoid.
```

> **Why it works:** restricting to provided info prevents invented competitor facts.

## 5. Customer persona from interview notes

```text
Here are notes from {n} customer interviews:
{notes}

Build 2 personas. For each: role, goals, pains (with direct quotes from the notes),
buying triggers, objections, and the channels where they look for solutions.
Only include traits supported by at least 2 interviews.
```

> **Why it works:** the "at least 2 interviews" rule keeps personas evidence-based.
